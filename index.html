The Reality

Every EEOC charge lands on your desk with the same math.

1

The Work Can't Be Delegated

20 to 30 hours of work per charge, a record that will follow the case into litigation, and a deadline that doesn't move – regardless of what else is on your plate, you're doing most of it yourself.

2

The Record Has to Be Right

The facts have to be right, the tone has to be right, and the legal framing has to hold up. There's no room for error on a document that travels into litigation.

3

The Support Options Haven't Solved This

Paralegals handle formatting, not legal reasoning. Outside counsel bills $5,000 to $30,000 and needs three orientation calls. General AI produces fluent text with no traceable basis for the facts asserted. The work comes back to you anyway.
